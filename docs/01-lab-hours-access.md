# Local AI Lab Opening Hours and Access

## Facility

The **Local AI Project Lab** is room **B-214** in the Faculty of Computer Engineering building. The lab hosts Foundry Local workstations for the summer RAG track.

## Opening hours

| Day | Hours | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| Monday–Friday | 09:00–18:00 | Staffed mentoring 14:00–16:00 |
| Saturday | 10:00–14:00 | Self-study only, no mentoring |
| Sunday | Closed | Remote work allowed on personal laptops |

Public holidays follow the university calendar. If a holiday falls on a weekday, the lab is closed and mentoring moves to chat.

## Access rules

1. Bring your student ID. Guests are not admitted without prior email approval.
2. Workstations are first-come, first-served except for reserved demo slots.
3. Demo-day reservations open every Wednesday at 12:00 on the lab booking form.
4. Food and drinks (except closed water bottles) are not allowed near GPU/NPU desks.
5. Save work to your GitHub repository before leaving. Lab disks are wiped every Sunday at 22:00.

## Remote access

VPN username format: `stu.<studentid>`. MFA is mandatory. VPN does **not** stream the lab GPU; use Foundry Local on your own machine for offline RAG work.
