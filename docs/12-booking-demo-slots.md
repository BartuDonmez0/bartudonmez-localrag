# Workstation Booking and Demo Slot Rules

## Booking window

Demo slots for room B-214 open every **Wednesday at 12:00**. Each slot is **20 minutes**: 5 minutes setup, 10 minutes demo, 5 minutes mentor questions.

## Slot limits

- Maximum **one** primary slot per student team per week.
- Teams of 2 share a single slot and must both appear on camera for remote demos.
- If you miss your slot without 12 hours notice, you go to the waitlist.

## On-site checklist before the slot

1. Arrive 10 minutes early.
2. Have your GitHub URL and video draft (even unfinished) ready.
3. Pre-load the model once before the slot so download progress is not part of the demo.
4. Keep a backup offline laptop if the lab PC fails.

## Remote demo alternative

If you cannot attend B-214, send a recorded 3-minute video plus a live link for Q&A during the weekly group call. Recording alone without answering mentor questions may receive at most **80%** of the demo category points.
